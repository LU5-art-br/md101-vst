🖼️ Graphical Interface & Controls

Built with a Dark Studio aesthetic, luminescent neon accents, and high-contrast typography.

🧪

118-Preset Periodic Table

The upper half features the full periodic table with authentic chemical family color coding:

  • One-Click Preset Loading: Click any cell to load its synthesized sonic preset immediately.
  • Hover & Preview: Hovering over any element previews its properties in the center HUD.
  • Active Frame & LED: Selected element is framed with a glowing border, active dot, and audio-reactive aura.
  • Interactive Legend: Hovering over any of the 10 categories at the top highlights all corresponding elements.
📊

Center HUD & Real-Time Scope

The central display provides physical atomic telemetry and live waveform analysis:

  • Atomic Card: Displays Atomic Number (Z), Symbol, localized name, Atomic Mass, and STP phase.
  • Telemetry Meters: Shows Harmonic Richness, Spectral Brightness, Body & Inertia, and Resonance & Release.
  • Live Stereo Oscilloscope: Renders the precise waveform generated by active polyphonic voices.
  • Sequential Navigation: Quick < and > buttons to browse presets sequentially.
🌐

Top Bar & Language Selector

Global controls and studio utilities with de-zippered anti-click smoothing:

  • Language Switcher (EN | PT): Instant toggle between English and Portuguese, saved with your DAW session.
  • Master Volume: Smooth linear output gain control preventing digital clicks and overs.
  • Ambience / Reverb: Regulates spatial reverb depth and stereo chorus spreading.
  • Voice & Polyphony Monitor (MIDI 0/16): Real-time active voice count (up to 16) and status LED colored by the active chemical family.
    • Instant diagnostic ("Where is the sound?"): if you play and it stays at 0/16, MIDI is not reaching the plugin; if it rises to ≥ 1/16 and lights up, MIDI is fine and you only need to check Master Volume or audio output.
    • Hanging notes: reveals if notes are still active when your DAW playback is stopped (clear with the Panic button).
    • Polyphony ceiling: reaching 16/16 shows the 16-voice maximum is reached and older voices are being smoothly recycled (*voice stealing*).

🎹 Virtual MIDI Keyboard & Performance Controls

Engineered for maximum playability, dynamic nuance, and live studio expression.

⚡

Vertical Velocity Sensitivity

The 61-key virtual keyboard detects the exact vertical position of your mouse click:

  • Bottom tip of key: Maximum velocity (127 - Forte / punchy attack transient).
  • Top area of key: Soft, intimate velocity (Piano / delicate touch).
  • Continuous Glissando: Click and drag across the keys horizontally for smooth sweeps.
⌨️

Computer QWERTY Keyboard Playback

Play melodies directly using your PC or laptop keyboard without requiring an external MIDI controller:

  • White Keys: A S D F G H J K L ;
  • Black Keys: W E T Y U O P
  • Octave Transpose: Z (down 1 octave) and X (up 1 octave).
🛡️

Performance Controls

Dedicated utility buttons designed for studio and live workflow:

  • Hold Button (Virtual Sustain): Latches triggered notes to sustain indefinitely for infinite pads and drones.
  • Panic Button: Dispatches an instant All Notes Off command and silences any hung voices.
  • - Octave and Octave +: Shifts the keyboard register across ±2 octaves (spanning C0 to C9).
  • DAW MIDI Feedback: Virtual keys light up in real time when notes are received from a DAW MIDI track.

🎛️ Installation Guide & DAW Setup

Compatible with all major professional digital audio workstations on Windows and Linux.

🪟

Windows Installation

Copy the built Md-101.vst3 bundle into your standard VST3 plugins folder:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Md-101.vst3

Or launch the Standalone desktop executable directly:

build\Md101_artefacts\Release\Standalone\Md-101.exe
🐧

Linux Installation

Copy the compiled Md-101.vst3 directory into your user's personal VST3 folder:

mkdir -p ~/.vst3
cp -r build/Md101_artefacts/Release/VST3/Md-101.vst3 ~/.vst3/

Then rescan VST3 plugins within your DAW of choice.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I automate element changes in my DAW?

In your DAW's MIDI track automation lane, enable automation for the `element` parameter (values 1 to 118). You can schedule presets to switch between song sections smoothly and without glitches!

A note is stuck sounding or there is no sound. How do I fix it?

Check the `MIDI n/16` indicator at the top: if it reads 0/16, MIDI events are not reaching the plugin from your DAW or keyboard. If it reads 1/16 or higher but you hear no sound, simply increase the Master Volume. If a note continues ringing indefinitely, click the Panic button in the footer to issue an immediate All Notes Off command and make sure Hold is not toggled on.

Does the synth consume heavy CPU resources?

No. The Md-101 synthesis engine is implemented in high-performance C++17 with analytic PolyBLEP oscillators and zero-delay feedback TPT SVF filters. CPU footprint is exceptionally low, allowing dozens of instances per session.

How does the language toggle work?

Click the EN | PT button in the top header. All element names, chemical families, telemetry labels, and status messages switch instantly between English and Portuguese, and your selection is persisted with the project.
